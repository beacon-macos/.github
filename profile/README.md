## What Beacon does

- **Opens from anywhere.** Use <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd>, record your own key combination, or double-tap <kbd>⌥</kbd>, <kbd>⌘</kbd>, <kbd>⌃</kbd>, or <kbd>⇧</kbd>.
- **Finds apps from a few letters.** Search ignores case and accents and matches letters in sequence, so `sset` finds System Settings.
- **Puts favorites first.** Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> to add one, and hold <kbd>⌥</kbd> with <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> to reorder them.
- **Falls back to the web.** When no app matches, the last result searches the web in your default browser.
- **Stays out of the way.** Beacon lives in the menu bar and has no Dock icon.
- **Stays native and private.** It is built with SwiftUI and AppKit, has no third-party dependencies, and keeps its app index on your Mac.

Beacon requires macOS 26 or later and runs on both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
